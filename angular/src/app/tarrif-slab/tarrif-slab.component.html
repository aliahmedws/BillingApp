How to add Tariff Slabs:
  • Lower & Upper slabs must be positive numbers.
  • Values support up to 9,999,999.999 with 4 decimals.
  • Upper slab must be greater than lower slab.
  • No duplicate slab ranges allowed.
  • Next lower slab starts 1 unit above previous upper slab.
  • Unit Price must be non-negative and not decrease across slabs.
